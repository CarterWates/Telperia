What Telperia measures

Capability, reliability, efficiency, and evidence.

TCI

Capability score across reasoning, coding, mathematics, factual knowledge, and instruction adherence.

Factual Reliability

Correctness, incorrect answer rate, abstention rate, and attempted accuracy for factual tasks.

Local IPW

Capability per watt-hour of measured local GPU energy during an evaluation run.

Verification

Evidence level, methodology version, hardware metadata, telemetry source, and limitations.

For builders

Run local evaluations and compare real configurations.

Builders can use the local runner to generate standardized packages, validate them privately, and prepare results for future public review.

For teams

Evaluation reports for AI decisions.

Telperia can grow into transparent reports covering model capability, reliability, latency, local energy use, hardware tradeoffs, and verification.
