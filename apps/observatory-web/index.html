Measurement philosophy

AI performance should not collapse into one unexplained number.

01 Capability

What can the model complete under a controlled benchmark suite?

02 Trust

How reliable are its factual answers, and what evidence backs the run?

03 Efficiency

What local GPU energy was consumed to produce that measured capability?

Methodology

Versioned methods, inspectable outputs.

Telperia scores are useful only when the measurement process is visible. Current MVP packages preserve methodology version, benchmark suite, hardware, telemetry source, energy scope, and verification level.

TCI v0.1

Capability score across reasoning, coding, mathematics, factual knowledge, and instruction adherence.

Factual Reliability

Correct, incorrect, abstained, and attempted-accuracy rates for factual tasks.

Local IPW v0.1

Capability per watt-hour of local GPU energy. Hosted and data-center IPW remain separate future work.

Hardware-aware evaluation

The machine is part of the result.

Telperia treats hardware, monitor backend, operating system, and energy scope as first-class metadata. Current measured seed results use a Windows RTX 5070 with NVML.

Windows NVIDIA Measured Local IPW with NVML
Linux NVIDIA Supported when NVML is available
Mac local development Capability runs; Local IPW deferred
Hosted/datacenter Future methodology; not mixed with Local IPW
